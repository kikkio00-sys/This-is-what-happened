Welcome

Every family has people who become its foundation. For the Zahner family, Gary and Brenda created a home where hard work, imagination, laughter, and generosity shaped everyday life. Their influence continues through their children, grandchildren, and great-grandchildren.

Gary Paul Zahner

Artist • Craftsman • Woodworker • Father • Grandfather

Gary's working life was shaped by creativity and craftsmanship. In retirement, woodworking became one of the clearest ways he expressed love for his family. His workshop produced keepsake boxes, birdhouses, mosaic designs, and personalized Christmas presents made by hand.

Brenda Joyce Downing Zahner

Mother • Grandmother • Human Resources • Retail Management

Brenda was intelligent, hardworking, loving, and often the life of the party. She worked in Human Resources at Presbyterian Hospital in Oklahoma City and for 7-Eleven, managed men's clothing stores, and later worked with an estate sale company. Her family remembers how she made ordinary days fun.
